modules and the same two coefficient rows. See the source hashes in its JSON.
No third-party implementation source or binary is redistributed here.

NumPy 2.2.6 (BSD-3-Clause) and fluids 1.3.1 (MIT) are dependencies.
Upstream license records remain in the isolated distributions; the inherited
requirements pins also retain the prior benchmark stack (including teqp),
though this compressor reference does not call teqp. No runtime network/data lookup.
