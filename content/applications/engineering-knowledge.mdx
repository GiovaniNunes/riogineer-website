## What engineering problem does it address?

Engineering organizations accumulate knowledge in technical specifications, engineering procedures, design practices, standards, simulation models, previous projects and engineering experience. This information can be distributed across documents, tools and teams.

## How does it work?

Specialized AI can help organize this information into structured and reusable engineering workflows. The Digital Engineer concept connects engineering knowledge with physical models, simulation and specialized software.
