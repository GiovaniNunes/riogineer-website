## What engineering problem does it address?

Process engineering requires the translation of technical requirements into process configurations, equipment models and simulation inputs. The Digital Engineer concept assists engineers in structuring these steps and interacting with specialized engineering tools.

## How does it work?

The conceptual workflow connects requirements extraction, process topology, a Process Flow Diagram (PFD), material and energy balances, equipment models and process simulation. AI provides interpretation and orchestration; physical models and engineering software provide the calculations.

## What information does it require?

An engineering specification is the starting point. The actual input specification, assumptions, boundary conditions and required data for a particular implementation are **[CONTENT REQUIRED]**.

## What engineering models are involved?

The workflow includes mass balances, energy balances and equipment models. The implemented model equations, supported equipment and simulator interfaces are **[CONTENT REQUIRED]**.

## What are the outputs and limitations?

The intended outputs include process configurations, engineering models and simulation results. Verified outputs, supported operating ranges, validation evidence and implementation-specific limitations are **[CONTENT REQUIRED]**.
