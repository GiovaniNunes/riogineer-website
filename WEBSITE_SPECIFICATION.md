# RIOGINEER WEBSITE — DEVELOPMENT SPECIFICATION

Version 1.1  
Website language: English only

---

# 1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Create the official corporate website for RIOGINEER LTDA.

RIOGINEER is a technology company focused on Artificial Intelligence applied to engineering.

The central concept is the development of specialized "Digital Engineers" capable of understanding engineering information, interacting with physical and mathematical models, executing simulations, generating engineering deliverables and supporting technical decisions.

The website must position RIOGINEER as an engineering technology and deeptech company, not as a traditional engineering consultancy.

The website should communicate:

- Engineering
- Artificial Intelligence
- Mathematical and physical modeling
- Industrial technology
- Technical reliability
- Scientific credibility
- Software engineering
- Industrial digitalization

The website must be appropriate for international B2B clients.

Initial target industries include:

- Oil & Gas
- Offshore engineering
- Process engineering
- Reservoir engineering

The architecture should allow future expansion into other engineering-intensive industries, including renewable energy, chemical processing, energy systems and infrastructure.

---

# 2. LANGUAGE

The entire website must be written in English.

Do NOT implement multilingual support in Version 1.

English must be used for navigation, page content, technical articles, demonstrations, metadata, SEO content, calls to action, contact forms and video descriptions.

Multilingual functionality may be added in the future, but it is outside the scope of Version 1.

---

# 3. CORPORATE DISTINCTION

RIOGINEER LTDA is a new technology company.

RIOGINEER and Rio Petróleo are separate companies.

Do NOT present RIOGINEER as another name for Rio Petróleo.

Rio Petróleo has previous engineering experience, technologies, software and industrial projects.

RIOGINEER may interact with or integrate engineering technologies made available through appropriate agreements.

One important example is Field Design.

The website may explain that RIOGINEER Digital Engineers can interact with engineering software such as Field Design.

However:

RIOGINEER and Field Design must NOT be presented as the same product.

Field Design is an engineering calculation, conceptual design and optimization platform.

RIOGINEER is an AI-powered Digital Engineer capable of interacting with engineering knowledge, physical models, simulators and specialized engineering software.

Do not make unsupported statements regarding ownership, licensing, intellectual property transfer or commercial agreements between RIOGINEER and Rio Petróleo.
